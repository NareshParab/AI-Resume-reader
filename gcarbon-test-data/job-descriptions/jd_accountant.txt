Job Title: Staff Accountant

We are seeking a Staff Accountant to manage accounts payable/receivable and support the monthly close process for a mid-sized manufacturing company.

Requirements:
- Bachelor's degree in Accounting or Commerce
- 2+ years of accounting experience
- Proficiency with accounting software (Tally, QuickBooks, or similar)
- Strong Excel skills including pivot tables
- Experience with GST/tax filing and bank reconciliation

Responsibilities include full-cycle AP/AR, monthly financial reporting, and supporting statutory compliance.
