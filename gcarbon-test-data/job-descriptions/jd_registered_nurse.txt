Job Title: Registered Nurse - Medical-Surgical Unit

We are seeking a licensed Registered Nurse to join our medical-surgical unit. The ideal candidate has strong patient assessment skills, experience with IV therapy and wound care, and is comfortable working in a fast-paced hospital environment.

Requirements:
- Valid RN license
- 2+ years of medical-surgical or acute care experience
- Proficiency with electronic health records (Epic or similar)
- BLS/ACLS certification
- Strong communication skills for patient and family education
- Ability to work rotating 12-hour shifts

Responsibilities include direct patient care, medication administration, care plan coordination with physicians, and mentoring newer staff.
