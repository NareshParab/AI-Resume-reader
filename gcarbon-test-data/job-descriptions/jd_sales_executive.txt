Job Title: B2B Account Executive

We're hiring an Account Executive to manage and grow a book of mid-market accounts. You'll own the full sales cycle from prospecting through close, using a consultative selling approach.

Requirements:
- 3+ years of B2B sales experience
- Proven track record of meeting or exceeding quota
- Experience with a CRM (Salesforce preferred)
- Strong negotiation and account management skills
- Comfortable with cold outreach and pipeline management

This role is quota-carrying with uncapped commission. Prior experience selling into industrial, manufacturing, or SaaS accounts is a plus.
